# 1971–1972 C10 rear two-tone coverage

The rear three-quarter center band now reaches the lower side molding and the bottom of the painted tailgate, including the strip beneath the Chevrolet insert. The correction is registered only for the 1971–1972 C10 group; legacy 1971 and 1972 links resolve to that same pack.

The existing source artwork and paint layers remain intact. A native SVG mask extension uses the current paint mask to exclude the chrome, tail lamps, insert, wheels and background. The extension follows the body offsets already used by the stance packs: stock 0, 2-inch lower 10, 4-inch lower 20 and laying frame 48 pixels. Every wheel choice uses the same corrected coverage.

Validation: TypeScript and all 60 existing tests pass. Stock and all three lowered stances were visually checked in the browser. The correction is confined to the two-tone mask; solid paint and the other views retain their existing rendering.
