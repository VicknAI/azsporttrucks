# 1969–1970 K10 upper-body paint coverage

The side-view body mask feathered out before reaching the upper chrome molding, exposing a thin red/orange line from the original artwork. Three native SVG contours now fill that missing coverage along the front fender, door and bed. The contours keep a continuous edge along the molding and preserve the separate panel seams.

The correction applies to the shared 1969–1970 K10 side pack and all five wheel choices. Existing raster artwork, roof, center-band and Rocker masks remain unchanged. Other angles and other vehicle packs retain their existing rendering.

Validation includes the original muted blue, the new #1882AF preset, solid charcoal and green Rocker paint. Render comparisons confirm the same bounded correction for all five wheel choices and no changed pixels outside the three contours. TypeScript and the 60 existing tests pass; the corrected side view was also checked in the browser.
