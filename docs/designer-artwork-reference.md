# Designer artwork reference

## 1968 C10 reference

Nick supplied this [1968 C10 listing](https://bringatrailer.com/listing/1968-chevrolet-c10-pickup-151/) on September 9, 2026. The inspected main photo shows solid blue paint including the roof and pillars, a Fleetside bed, horizontal grille bars with a center bowtie, round headlights, hood-front Chevrolet lettering, rectangular side markers, chrome bumpers, round mirrors, and a lowered stance. Use these visible details for the separate 1968 artwork study; confirm additional angles against the gallery before preparing masks.

This is a modified LS3-powered truck with aftermarket 20-inch Schott wheels and LED lighting per the listing. Its modifications are build inspiration, not verification of factory equipment or wheel fitment. Blue is a visual reference, not a verified paint code. The 1968 studio pack remains pending; no listing photos have been added to the public website.

## 1971–1972 K10 two-tone reference

Nick supplied this [1972 K10 Cheyenne listing](https://bringatrailer.com/listing/1972-chevrolet-k10-pickup-36/) for both 1971 and 1972 K10 paint studies. The listing describes Medium Red and white paint. The inspected rear three-quarter photo shows a broad white center band continuing onto the tailgate, red upper bed rails and lower body, chrome moldings defining the band, and a red cab roof and pillars. Preserve this body-color cab option separately from the earlier C10 white-cab reference. The black tailgate insert, lettering, chrome bumper, and lights stay outside the paint masks.

The pictured truck has a modified lifted stance; its suspension, wheels and tires are reference-specific, not standard equipment for every K10. This 1972 example is a user-selected paint reference for both years, not verification that every year-specific detail is identical. Reference captured September 9, 2026; K10 studio artwork remains pending.

## C10 artwork

The 1971 C10 study uses the owner-supplied [Bring a Trailer listing](https://bringatrailer.com/listing/1971-chevrolet-c10-pickup-187/) as an authoring reference. Listing photos are not website assets.

Paint layout: broad white center band between side moldings, orange upper and lower panels, and white cab roof plus painted window pillars. The designer also offers lower-body two-tone and roof-only contrast. These choices are available for C10/K10; colors are visual approximations, not factory codes.

1967 and 1971 C10 each have separate generated studio artwork for all four views. Other years/models retain labeled schematics. Generated grille, trim, wheel details and bed proportions are not factory verified. In particular, the 1971 side study appears longer than the short-bed reference and needs further artwork refinement. Wheel product choices remain summary-only in studio views.
