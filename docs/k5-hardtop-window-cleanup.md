# K5 hardtop window paint cleanup

The 1969–1970 and 1971–1972 K5 groups use `color-v6/top-on` artwork.
The roof masks now include the white fiberglass reveals that were excluded
around the side and rear window seals. These areas follow body-color, black,
and white roof selections, using corrected hardtop shading.

The original studio images, body and two-tone masks, glass, black seals, wheels,
and interior are preserved. The top-off configuration still uses its v5 pack.
All 32 alternate wheel scenes share the same unmodified upper artwork.

Reproducible preparation and visual comparisons are in the workspace sibling
`assets/k5-roof-refinement-v6/refine-roof.py`. Its checks protect body paint,
dark seals, and window interiors. Review covers all four views of both front-end
families with bronze, green, graphite satin, black, and white hardtops.
