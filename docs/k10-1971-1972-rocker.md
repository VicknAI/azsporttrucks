# 1971–1972 K10 Rocker two-tone

September 24, 2026. Nick requested a **Rocker** option alongside Center band, starting with the paired 1971–1972 K10. Secondary paint begins below the bottom edge of the lower body molding. Upper panels remain the main body color, and the existing contrasting cab option stays independent.

Four new `rocker-mask.png` files live under `public/designer/studio/chevrolet-k10-1972-color-v4/{view}/`. Native polygons follow the lower trim in each view and are intersected with the existing body mask. This includes the narrow painted tailgate strip below the applique and above the rear bumper. Straight-front coverage is intentionally empty because the bumper hides the lower body panels. Chrome, wheels, tires, glass and background are excluded.

Only this group's studio packs advertise `rockerPaint`. Saved drafts, shared links, exports and quote reviews retain Rocker; legacy individual 1971/1972 shared IDs still normalize to the paired group. Other vehicles keep their existing pattern choices. Center band remains the default, and selecting Solid ignores the secondary paint layer.

The native build, verification and four-view previews are in workspace `assets/k10-1971-1972-rocker/`. All 24 pre-existing layer files remain byte-identical. An independent visual review checks blue/white and white/red contrast treatments, plus enlarged trim details.
