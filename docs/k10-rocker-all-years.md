# Rocker two-tone for every K10 year group

All eleven K10 selections from 1967 through 1987 now offer **Center band** and
**Rocker** under Paint & finish → Two-tone pattern. Center band remains the
default. Switching years retains Rocker; legacy individual 1969–1972 shared links
still resolve to their paired years. Cab contrast and wheel choices remain independent.

Forty new `rocker-mask.png` files extend the existing 1971–1972 support. Each mask
uses the selected pack's 768×512 geometry and is clipped to its approved body-paint
alpha. The secondary paint starts below the lower molding, or the lower body
crease on 1967–1968. Narrow painted strips below the tailgate are included, with
the bumper, applique and Chevrolet lettering protected. Straight-front masks are
empty because the bumper conceals the lower sheet metal.

The current 1971–1972 masks and all 268 pre-existing files in the selected K10
packs are unchanged. Every pack retains its existing source scene, center-band,
roof/cab layers and neutral texture. Wheel scenes use the same aligned paint masks.

Native authoring, three high-contrast four-view reviews per group and hash checks
live in workspace `assets/k10-rocker-all-years/`. Regression coverage exercises all
eleven groups, year transitions, cab contrast, wheel choices, shared configurations,
offline exports and private quote reviews. A Rocker selection with Solid paint
does not apply the secondary color.
